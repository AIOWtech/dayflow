Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Dayflow handles information when you use our website, workspace, and connected features.

Effective date: 22 November 2026
Dayflow is operated by AIOWtech. We aim to use only the information needed to provide the service, keep your workspace available, and make connected features work. Please do not submit passwords, payment card details, or other highly sensitive information in task instructions.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: information you provide when registering or managing your account, such as your name, email address, phone number (if used), and optional profile details.
  • Workspace content: task instructions, plans, project information, documents, generated results, task status, and activity records you create or save in Dayflow.
  • Connected Google account information: when you choose to connect Google, we receive basic account identity information such as your Google email address and authorization tokens, together with the permissions you approve.
  • Service and device information: technical information that may be recorded by our hosting and security services, such as request details, timestamps, browser information, and error or diagnostic logs.
  • Support communications: information you include when contacting us for help.

2. How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts; save and display your workspace; carry out tasks you request; generate plans, research summaries, and documents; prepare emails; support Google-connected email and file workflows; troubleshoot errors; prevent abuse; and communicate important service or policy updates.

3. AI features and third-party services

To provide Dayflow, relevant task instructions or content may be processed by service providers that help us operate the product. Depending on the feature you use, these may include:

  • Supabase for authentication and workspace database storage.
  • OpenRouter and the selected AI model provider to generate plans, research summaries, documents, or email drafts. Content you submit for an AI task may be sent for processing.
  • Tavily to retrieve web search results for research tasks.
  • Browserbase to provide an isolated browser session for reading public webpages when you request a browser task.
  • Vercel for application hosting and delivery.
  • Google when you connect your Google account and use the related features.

These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. We do not sell your personal information. We do not allow these providers to use your Dayflow content for unrelated purposes on our behalf.

4. Google permissions and connected services

If you connect Google, Dayflow requests permissions for basic sign-in identity and the specific Google features currently supported, including sending Gmail messages and working with files created or opened by Dayflow in Google Drive. We use those permissions to provide the feature you choose. Dayflow does not send an email merely because a draft is generated: you must review and approve an email before the approval-based send workflow proceeds.

You can disconnect Google from Dayflow's Integrations area. You can also review or revoke Dayflow's access in your Google Account security settings. Disconnecting Dayflow does not itself delete information already stored in your Google account.

5. Browser tasks

When you request a browser task, Dayflow may send the public HTTPS address you provide to its browser service to retrieve and summarize visible page content. The current browser workflow is designed for read-only inspection; it is not intended to sign in to third-party accounts or submit forms on your behalf.

6. Storage, security, and retention

Workspace data is stored using our database provider and is associated with your account. Google refresh tokens used for connected features are encrypted before storage. We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, including access controls and transport security. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

We keep account and workspace information while your account is active and as needed to provide the service, meet legitimate operational needs, resolve disputes, and comply with applicable law. Some information may remain temporarily in backups or security logs after deletion. We do not promise a fixed deletion period unless we publish one separately.

7. Your choices and rights

You can access and update certain account information in Dayflow, choose whether to connect Google, and stop using individual AI or connected features. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information by contacting us. Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have additional privacy rights. We may need to verify your request before acting on it.

8. International processing

Our service providers may process or store information in countries other than the country where you live. Where required, we take steps intended to support appropriate safeguards for such transfers.

9. Children and young users

Dayflow is not designed for children under 13. If you are under 18, use Dayflow only with the involvement and permission of a parent or legal guardian. If you believe a child has provided personal information in a way that is not permitted, contact us so we can review the request.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Dayflow changes. We will update the effective date above and, where appropriate, provide notice through the service.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the Dayflow team at aiowtechs@gmail.com.

This policy describes the current intended operation of Dayflow and may be updated as features, providers, and legal requirements change.
